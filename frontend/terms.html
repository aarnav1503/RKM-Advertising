Terms of Service & Policies

Please review our official editorial acceptance rules, cancellation deadlines, and data privacy policies.

1. Editorial Policy & Publication Discretion

RKM Ads (BookMyAd clone) functions as an accredited booking facilitator for print, broadcast, and digital media. The final acceptance, typesetting, and placement of any advertisement rest solely with the editorial desk of the respective publication or broadcaster.

2. Mandatory Documentation for Legal Notices

Advertisements falling under categories including Change of Name, Court Notices, Loss of Documents/Shares, Public Notices, and Obituaries require mandatory legal documents (such as notarized affidavits, police FIR acknowledgments, or death certificates). Failure to submit compliant documents before the edition cut-off time may result in postponement of the release date.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellations Prior to Cut-off: Orders canceled at least 24 hours prior to the publication's official cut-off time are eligible for a 90% refund (10% standard processing fee deducted).
  • Post Cut-off Cancellations: Once an advertisement has been sent to the printing press, plate-casted, or scheduled for broadcast, cancellations cannot be accepted.
  • Editorial Rejections: In the rare event that an advertisement is rejected by the publication's legal desk, a 100% full refund is credited back to the original payment source within 3–5 working days.

4. Privacy & Data Security

We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions. Customer phone numbers, email addresses, and uploaded legal identification documents are stored securely and never shared with third parties, except as mandated by law or required by the publishing houses for verification.
