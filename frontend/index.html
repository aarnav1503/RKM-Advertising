Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for a Change of Name ad?

Newspapers require a copy of a notarized affidavit (attested within the last 6 months) and a valid government ID proof. Upload them during Step 4 before payment.

How does the word vs. line counting formula work?

The Times of India charges on a per-line basis (standard ~35 characters per line). Hindi publications like Dainik Jagran charge per word. The live calculator handles this math automatically as you type.

What is the publication cut-off time?

Most national newspapers close their next-day editions by 3:00 PM. Weekend matrimonial sections close on Friday afternoon. The cut-off timer will automatically guide your date selection.

Can I obtain a GST invoice for tax credit?

Yes. Enter your GSTIN during checkout, and a compliant tax invoice will be generated along with your booking confirmation.
